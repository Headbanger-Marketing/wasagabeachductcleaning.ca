Sending one identical request and question list to each provider compared

Asking several providers the same thing only works if the replies can be set side by side. Write one request pack covering your Wasaga Beach home: the rooms, the registers and return grilles you can count, the dryer vent route, any HRV or ERV, and the equipment labels. Add photos and access notes, then a fixed list of questions about scope, method, exclusions and the job record. If each provider answers the same questions, differences in scope become visible.

  1. Return grilles

    Where room air is drawn back toward the furnace.

  2. Return ducts

    The runs leading from each grille to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    Blower assembly, coil and the cabinet around them.

  4. Supply trunk

    The main duct leaving the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    Smaller ducts feeding each room.

  6. Supply registers

    The floor, wall or ceiling vents in each room.

Questions About Your Wasaga Beach Request

Does Wasaga Beach Duct Cleaning do any of the duct work it takes requests for?

The site carries out no duct work itself, since it is an enquiry website for Wasaga Beach and not a contractor. Our enquiry team reviews each request and may pass it to a provider, though nothing on the site confirms who that provider would be. That provider must identify itself, confirm it covers your address and put its scope in writing before anything is arranged.

Who gets the request pack I send for my Wasaga Beach home, and do they choose a provider?

Our enquiry team is where the pack goes: sending it places your request in front of that team for review. Sending does not book anything, name a provider, confirm coverage or fix a price. If a provider responds, the replies are yours to compare, and you decide whether to go further with any of them. No reply is ranked or recommended for you. Keep a copy of the pack so you can reuse it.

Why send the same photos to each provider for my Wasaga Beach home?

Different photos lead providers to answer different questions, so their replies cannot be lined up. One photo set of the registers, the dryer connector, the outside hoods and any equipment labels gives each provider the same starting point. Differences in the replies then come from the providers' scope and method rather than from what each was shown.

What questions should a numbered list include for a duct cleaning request?

Cover scope, method, exclusions and the job record. Ask which ducts, registers and other components are included, how loosened debris is collected, what is left out and why, and what written record, with photos, is handed over afterwards. Number the questions once and reuse the same numbers for every provider you ask.

How can I set out replies from different providers in a simple table?

Put the question numbers down the left side and one column for each provider. In each cell, copy the answer in the provider's own words, or write not answered. Leave out any ranking. A blank or vague cell is a prompt for a follow-up question, not a score against that provider. Dates, names and written scope can go in extra rows.

Can one request pack cover both the dryer vent and the ducts?

It can, if the dryer exhaust, the furnace ducts and any HRV each have their own heading. Ask each provider to answer the same questions for each heading and to list any item it leaves out, so a missing vent or unit shows up as a gap rather than a surprise.

What if one provider's reply is much shorter than the others?

A shorter reply is a difference to explain, not a verdict. Ask the provider what it left out, whether the scope is narrower or simply less detailed, and what it would add if asked the same numbered questions. Do not treat the lowest total as the right choice; a lower figure may cover fewer sections or leave items on separate lines.

Who decides which provider to use once replies for my Wasaga Beach home come back?

You do, together with anyone who must authorize the work, such as a landlord, property manager or condominium corporation. Our enquiry team does not choose for you. Before agreeing, confirm who does the work, the written scope, the pricing and the timing, and ask about qualifications and insurance. Keep the completed table with the written scope.
